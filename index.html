Forest Biometrics & Inventory Modeling

Forest inventory, growth & yield, forest mensuration, small area estimation, mixed-effects and machine-learning models on plot data, forest resources management

Your forest inventory tells you what a handful of plots look like. I build the statistical models — small area estimation, mixed-effects, machine learning — that turn those plots into reliable biomass and volume estimates for every county, stand, or parcel you manage. Forestry is where I started, and it still frames how I read every dataset.

M.S. Forest Biometrics, GPA 4.0 — thesis on small area estimation for county-level forest biomass

Enterprise & Operational GIS

ArcGIS Pro / Online / Enterprise Portal, geodatabase and schema design, feature & REST services, spatial data management, GIS QA/QC

A forest inventory, a utility network, a research archive — none of it holds up without the GIS underneath. I design the geodatabase schemas, publish the feature and REST services, and set up the ArcGIS Enterprise Portal access and QA that let a whole team work from the same spatial data.

Designed schemas and published feature services on an ArcGIS Enterprise Portal for FPL

Remote Sensing & LiDAR

Satellite & LiDAR remote sensing, image interpretation, change detection, canopy & vegetation monitoring, land-cover classification

No field crew can walk every acre of canopy or every mile of utility corridor. I work with LiDAR and satellite imagery to detect, classify, and monitor change — vegetation structure, land cover, encroachment — at a scale field work can't match.
